How this strategy works

A rules-based swing-trading test on an Alpaca paper account. Each signal is traded two ways and the results are kept separate: stock, and an at-the-money call.

Setups

  • Three White Soldiers pullback (monthly bars, quarterly trend filter): three strong up bars, then a shallow dip into the middle bar's range, then a bullish bar that closes above the prior high.
  • Mother Bar Breakout (monthly bars with quarterly trend, quarterly bars with yearly trend, and weekly bars with monthly trend): a wide-range bar followed by 1–8 inside bars, then a green bar closing above the wide bar's high while the higher-timeframe trend points up.
  • Shakeout Breakout (weekly bars, monthly trend filter): price pokes above a 52-bar high and fails, then closes above it on above-average volume, and the next bar confirms with a close above the breakout bar's high.

Rules

  • A signal counts only after its bar has closed. New orders are placed once a day, after the close. The 15-minute passes during market hours only manage fills and exits.
  • Entry: a buy-stop just above the signal bar's high. Skipped if price has already traded through it, if the planned reward is less than 3× the risk, or if the stock wasn't in the Nasdaq-100 on the signal date.
  • Stop: below the pattern (mother bar low, first soldier's low, or breakout-bar low).
  • Exits: half at target 1 (prior major high, or 3R if there is none), one fifth at target 2 (1.618× the target-1 distance), and the rest when the 5-period EMA closes below the 13-period EMA on the trading timeframe.
  • Unfilled orders are cancelled when the next bar on the same timeframe closes.
  • Sizing: 2% of the track's equity at risk per trade, at most 20% of the track in one position, at most 10 positions per track.
  • Calls: bought only after the stock order fills. Expiry nearest about 180 days for weekly signals and about 400 days for monthly or quarterly ones, nearest strike, bid/ask spread ≤ 10%, open interest ≥ 50. At most one contract when its premium is within 10% of the call track; otherwise skipped. Call exits follow the stock exits.
