Backtest results (2016–2025)

  • Universe: point-in-time Nasdaq-100. A stock trades only while it is an index member, which removes survivorship bias.
  • Data: daily Yahoo prices.
  • Costs: 5 bps slippage per side, $0.005 per share, and 10 bps extra on stop fills.
  • Sizing: $100k start, 2% risk per trade, at most 10 positions. Only setups with at least 20 trades are shown.
  • Options: priced with a Black-Scholes model (implied volatility estimated from realized volatility). These are not historical option fills.

Takeaways: - No setup beat buy-and-hold over this period. The weekly and monthly setups made about 7–10% a year, with shallower drawdowns than the index. - Daily-timeframe versions made 0–5% after costs. Short versions lost money. - Modeled at-the-money calls improved several monthly results, but deepened drawdowns. - 15-minute versions of the same entries lost money after costs.
