Setup rules

All setups are long-only. Each uses two timeframes: a trend timeframe one step higher sets direction, and a trading timeframe produces the signal. Indicators are standard: EMAs 5/13/26/50, MACD 12/26/9, RSI 14, Stochastic 14/3/3, Bollinger Bands 20/2, ADX 14, and 20-bar average volume.

Setup Trend filter (higher timeframe) Signal (trading timeframe) Stop Target 1
Three White Soldiers pullback none 3 up bars closing in their top third with rising closes, each with a body of at least 50% of its range and at least 0.5× ATR(14); within 1–3 bars a dip into bar 2's range; a green trigger bar closing above the prior high low of bar 1 52-bar high, else 3R
Mother Bar Breakout 13 EMA > 26 EMA with close > 26 EMA, or MACD histogram rising bar with range ≥ 1.2 ATR, then 1–8 inside bars, then a green bar closing above its high mother bar low 52-bar high, else 3R
Shakeout Breakout none within 10 bars a failed poke above the 52-bar high; a breakout close above it on above-average volume; next bar closes above the breakout high (enter next open) breakout bar low 3R
Band Momentum Breakout close above the band middle, MACD histogram rising, and RSI > 50 a fresh close above the upper band (no close above it in the prior 3 bars; band rising), close above the 50 EMA, RSI > 60, volume above average, 5 EMA > 13 EMA, +DI > −DI, ADX > 15 and rising, two higher swing lows breakout bar low 52-bar high, else 3R
Double Bottom / Failed Breakdown low stays above the lower band second low at most 0.5× ATR(14) above the first (≥ 8 bars apart), or a brief undercut that closes back above the first; a bullish engulfing, piercing or morning-star bar, RSI > 40, stochastic turning up from below 35, low tags the lower band, volume above average lower of the two lows neckline + pattern height

Chart-only setups: Triangle Breakout, Wedge Divergence Reversal and Failed Breakdown Reclaim are shown on the chart indicator but are not traded by the paper bot. A rule review on Oct 26, 2026 found no edge in their backtests. Multi-Timeframe Pullback, Third-Wave Breakout and Continuation Divergence are also chart-only. The paper bot trades only Three White Soldiers, Mother Bar Breakout and Shakeout Breakout (see How it works).

Shared rules: the entry is a buy-stop at the signal bar's high, valid for the next bar only. Reward must be at least 3× risk. Exits are 50% at target 1, 20% at target 2, and 30% on the 5/13 EMA cross. Every rule uses only completed bars, so there is no look-ahead.
