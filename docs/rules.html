Setup rules

All setups are long-only. Each uses two timeframes: a trend timeframe one step higher sets direction, and a trading timeframe produces the signal. Indicators are standard: EMAs 5/13/26/50, MACD 12/26/9, RSI 14, Stochastic 14/3/3, Bollinger Bands 20/2, ADX 14, and 20-bar average volume.

Setup Trend filter (higher timeframe) Signal (trading timeframe) Stop Target 1
Three White Soldiers pullback none 3 up bars closing in their top third with rising closes; within 1–3 bars a dip into bar 2's range; a green trigger bar closing above the prior high low of bar 1 52-bar high, else 3R
Mother Bar Breakout 13 EMA > 26 EMA with close > 26 EMA, or MACD histogram rising bar with range ≥ 1.2 ATR, then 1–4 inside bars, then a close above its high mother bar low 52-bar high, else 3R
Shakeout Breakout none within 10 bars a failed poke above the 52-bar high; a breakout close above it on above-average volume; next bar closes above the breakout high (enter next open) breakout bar low 3R
Band Momentum Breakout close above the band middle and MACD histogram rising close above the upper band (band rising), RSI > 60, volume above average, 5 EMA > 13 EMA, +DI > −DI, ADX > 15 and rising, two higher swing lows breakout bar low 52-bar high, else 3R
Double Bottom / Failed Breakdown low stays above the lower band second low within 3% of the first (≥ 8 bars apart), or a brief undercut that closes back above; bullish bar, RSI > 40, stochastic turning up from below 35, low tags the lower band lower of the two lows neckline + pattern height

Shared rules: the entry is a buy-stop at the signal bar's high, valid for the next bar only. Reward must be at least 3× risk. Exits are 50% at target 1, 20% at target 2, and 30% on the 5/13 EMA cross. Every rule uses only completed bars, so there is no look-ahead.
