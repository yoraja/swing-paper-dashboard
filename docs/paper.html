How the paper runner works

  • 15-minute pass: runs every 15 minutes during market hours (06:30–13:00 PT, weekdays). It checks order status and, when a buy-stop fills:
  • places the stock exits: two one-cancels-other orders (target 1 or stop, target 2 or stop) plus a stop for the remaining 30%;
  • buys the call;
  • mirrors stock exits on the call.
  • End-of-day pass: runs at 13:45 PT on weekdays. It scans completed bars for new signals and places buy-stops, applies the EMA exit, and cancels buy-stops whose entry window has ended.
  • Duplicate protection: every order has a fixed client id built from symbol, setup, timeframe and signal date. Re-runs never place a second order.
  • Ledger: a trade ledger and a daily equity file per track feed this dashboard. The page republishes only when its content changes.
  • Safety: the paper endpoint is enforced, and the account is separate from other bots. Credentials are never written to logs or to this site.
